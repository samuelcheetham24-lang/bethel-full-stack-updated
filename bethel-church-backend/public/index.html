Our Mission

Faith without walls.

Bethel Divine Mystery Church exists to help people encounter God without pretense — no matter their background, denomination, or how long it's been since they last walked into a church. We believe the sacred isn't reserved for the certain; it meets the searching too. Our mission is simple: to build one family across Sowutuom and Nsakina where every person is known, every question is welcome, and every life is invited into transformation.

Radical Welcome

No tradition, background, or past required — just come. You belong here before you believe here.

Genuine Community

Faith was never meant to be walked alone. We do life together, across both our Sowutuom and Nsakina family.

Everyday Faith

Church isn't just Sunday. We help you carry what you encounter here into every ordinary day of your week.
